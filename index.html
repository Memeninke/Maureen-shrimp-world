The Mantis Shrimp

The mantis shrimp is one of the most fascinating creatures in the ocean, renowned for its vibrant colours and extraordinary hunting abilities. Neither a true shrimp nor a mantis, this marine crustacean belongs to the order Stomatopoda. They are divided into two main categories based on their hunting style: spearers and smashers.

The smashers possess incredibly powerful, club-like appendages that they use to strike prey. They can accelerate their clubs at the speed of a firing bullet, generating enough force to shatter aquarium glass and easily crack open the shells of crabs and snails. This strike is so fast that it creates cavitation bubbles, producing a secondary shockwave that can stun prey even if the physical strike misses.

Why The Mantis Shrimp Is Unique

  • They have Trinocular Vision.
  • They can recognise rivals.
  • Some species form lifelong partnerships.
